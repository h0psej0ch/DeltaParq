\section{Expected Results}

After the research has been executed, it is expected that it can be concluded if 
the hypothesis that DNS records remain mostly unchanged between OpenINTEL measurements 
was right. Furthermore, the research is expected to conclude which delta-compression 
strategies are favorable and unfavorable for both compression and analysis efficiency.
Finally, the research is expected to deliver a storage and conversion structure that 
will aid the OpenINTEL project in reducing their storage needs while improving their 
analysis efficiency.
